Our Mission

HardwareTest is a registered digital product studio initiative spearheaded by Kunwar Aryan Tomar. We build lightweight, client-side Micro-SaaS tools engineered to solve specific technical bottlenecks without the bloat of legacy websites.

Whether you are fine-tuning a custom PC build, ensuring your new ultra-high refresh rate monitor is actually outputting maximum frames, or verifying that your gaming mouse is genuinely polling at 1000Hz+, you need data you can trust instantly. That is why we built this diagnostic utility.

Why It's Free

We believe essential system diagnostics shouldn't be hidden behind paywalls, intrusive pop-ups, or mandatory software downloads. By keeping our architecture efficient and utilizing ad networks to cover operational costs, we are able to offer these high-precision tools completely free to users worldwide.
